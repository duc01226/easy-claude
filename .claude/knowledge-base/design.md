## Digest

- Ground the design in its subject matter; spend each free axis on a choice, not a default.
- The generated-design tell catalog is calibration, not prohibition: report "a free axis spent on a default", never a trait as a defect.
- The brief's own words always win.
- Plan before building (the design plan contract), then critique the built result.
- Usability floors (`UI-1.1`–`UI-9.4`) live in their own protocol; this catalog asks whether the interface is this product's own.
