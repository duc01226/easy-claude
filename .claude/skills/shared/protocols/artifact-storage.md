## Generated Artifact Storage

Store disposable generated output in the project workspace. Generated output is disposable unless its owning contract explicitly declares it a source-of-truth or an intentionally versioned projection: committed fixtures, accepted baselines, canonical specs/docs and explicitly versioned mirrors remain at their declared owner paths. Write temporary state, integration/E2E test results, reports, logs, screenshots, traces, videos, coverage, dumps, candidate evidence, and any other reproducible non-source output under the project-root `tmp/` or `temp/` directory (prefer `tmp/`), scoped to the run. The project-root `.gitignore` must ignore `/tmp/` and `/temp/` by default. Do not place disposable output in source, docs, generated mirror directories, or the plans and team-artifacts roots (defaults `plans/` and `team-artifacts/`; `docsRoots.plans.path` and `docsRoots.teamArtifacts.path` in `docs/project-config.json` override them). Local-only paths (`tmp/`, `temp/`, an ignored plans root, anything `git check-ignore` matches) are absent from a clean checkout: a tracked or shared file (tracker record, spec, doc, lesson, commit message, PR text) never cites one as a source, link or evidence. Carry the needed content into the file, cite the plan or record by its id, or first move the artifact to a committed home and cite that; naming a convention such as "reports go under `tmp/`" is not a citation.
